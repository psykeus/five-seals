Controls

← ↑ ↓ → or WASDMove
Z or JB: staff strike (free)
X or KA: cast the selected Art
ShiftSelect: cycle through the Arts you know
EnterStart: begin, pause, close text

Click the game screen first so it receives your keys. A USB or Bluetooth gamepad also works. On a phone, use the touch pad under the screen.

Route through the five seals

  1. Ember Temple. From the Sanctum, go north, then east twice to Bramble Pass. Raise the Veil (A) to walk through the rune rocks to the north. Inside, learn Fire and defeat the Gale Warden with it.
  2. Stone Temple. In the Old Wood (far northwest), burn the brambles in front of the cave. Learn Earth. Your boulder breaks cracked walls and fills pits. The Mire Serpent only takes damage from Earth.
  3. Sky Spire. Back at Bramble Pass, break the cracked rocks on the east edge. Go north to the spire. Learn Wind, a dash that crosses chasms. Dash into the Stone Colossus to damage it.
  4. Tide Temple. In the far southeast, dash across the chasm. Learn Water, which puts out walls of flame. The Cinder Drake only takes damage from Water.
  5. The Loom. With four seals, return to the Sanctum. The seal stone becomes a stair down. The Unweaver can only be hit while the Veil is up, and only with the Art that matches its current color.

Optional: the Trial Cave west of the Sanctum (Fire mastery), hearts on the southeast lake and in the Stone Temple, and Veil mastery in the Loom.

Saves happen automatically at the Sanctum, at every temple entrance, and when you pick up a relic. They're kept in this browser only.
